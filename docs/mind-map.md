Root: **Construction Business App** (Skyeline OS)

## 1. Subcontractor Portal
- **Work Execution:** Assigned Tasks · Scope of Work · Installation Progress · Crew Scheduling · Completion Updates
- **General Contractor Coordination:** Project Schedules · Look-Ahead Planning · Task Dependencies · Payment Applications · Change Requests
- **Designer Coordination:** Plans Review · Specification Checks · Detail Clarifications · Shop Drawings · Field Questions
- **Administrative Tools:** Contract Documents · Insurance Records · Compliance Uploads · Time Tracking · Invoices
- **Communication:** Messaging · Issue Reporting · Photo Uploads · Notifications · Punch List Updates

## 2. General Contractor Portal
- **Project Management:** Project Setup · Project Timeline · Milestones · Daily Logs · Site Progress Tracking
- **Budget & Financial Control:** Estimate Management · Budget Tracking · Cost Codes · Change Order Pricing · Invoice Oversight
- **Subcontractor Coordination:** Bid Management · Scope Assignment · Sub Scheduling · Workforce Coordination · Payment Processing
- **Client Management:** Project Status Updates · Budget Visibility · Approval Requests · Meeting Summaries · Issue Escalation
- **Document & Compliance Control:** Contracts · Permits · Plans and Drawings · RFIs · Submittals · Safety Records

## 3. Client Portal
- **Project Visibility:** Project Status · Project Timeline · Milestone Tracking · Photo Updates · Progress Summaries
- **Financial Oversight:** Budget Snapshot · Allowance Tracking · Change Order Review · Payment History · Approval Decisions
- **Design Participation:** Design Reviews · Material Selections · Finish Choices · Feedback Submissions · Decision Logs
- **Communication & Approvals:** Messaging · Meeting Notes · Approval Requests · Questions and Responses · Notifications
- **Document Access:** Contracts · Plans · Specifications · Warranties · Project Documents

## 4. Designer Portal
- **Design Management:** Concept Development · Design Packages · Drawing Revisions · Specification Management · Asset Schedules
- **Client Collaboration:** Design Reviews · Selection Management · Feedback Collection · Presentation Boards · Approval Tracking
- **Contractor Coordination:** Drawing Issuance · Clarifications · RFI Responses · Change Documentation · Site Observation Notes
- **Subcontractor Support:** Technical Details · Shop Drawing Review · Installation Guidance · Material Specifications · Field Clarifications
- **Document Control:** Plans and Details · Version History · Specification Library · Marked-Up Drawings · Approval Archives

## 5. Shared Workflows & Interactions
- **GC ↔ Client:** Project Status Sharing · Budget Visibility · Approval Workflows · Change Order Decisions · Progress Reporting · Financial Transparency
- **Designer ↔ Client:** Design Reviews · Selections Coordination · Feedback Loops · Approval of Design Choices · Revision Discussions · Expectation Alignment
- **GC ↔ Subcontractor:** Schedule Coordination · Task Assignment · Work Progress Updates · Payment Processing · Change Management · Field Issue Resolution
- **Designer ↔ Subcontractor:** Specifications Sharing · Plans Distribution · Detail Clarifications · Shop Drawing Collaboration · Installation Coordination · Technical Communication
- **All Four Portals:** Project Timeline Visibility · Change Tracking · Shared Documents · Central Communication Thread · Version-Controlled Plans · Decision History · Issue Tracking · Notifications and Alerts

## 6. Interconnected Platform Foundation
- **Unified Data Layer:** Single Source of Truth · Shared Project Records · Cross-Portal Updates · Real-Time Synchronization
- **Role-Based Access:** Portal-Specific Views · Permission Controls · Approval Rights · Secure Sharing
- **Collaboration Engine:** Comments and Mentions · Task Linking · Document Sharing · Workflow Routing
- **Audit & Traceability:** Activity Logs · Revision History · Approval Trails · Accountability Tracking
- **Automation & Alerts:** Deadline Reminders · Approval Notifications · Change Alerts · Payment Status Updates
