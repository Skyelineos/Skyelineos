1. Program Description

Skyeline Homes is a custom home builder based in American Fork, Utah. Our SMS program is used to coordinate active construction projects with clients, subcontractors, vendors, and inspectors. Messages include:

  • Project status updates (framing, concrete, drywall, inspections, milestones).
  • Schedule confirmations and changes.
  • Material delivery confirmations.
  • Task assignments and reminders.
  • Inspection notices.
  • Bid, invoice, and payment notifications.

Messages may be sent in English or Spanish to accommodate our bilingual subcontractor workforce.

2. How to Opt In

You can opt in to receive SMS messages from Skyeline Homes through one of the following methods:

  1. Construction Contract: Clients sign a written construction contract that includes explicit SMS consent language. By signing and providing your mobile number, you acknowledge that you will receive project-related SMS updates from Skyeline Homes at that number. Opt-in consent is documented separately on the contract; the SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default and is never required as a condition of receiving construction services.
  2. Subcontractor Onboarding: Subcontractors provide their mobile number when being added to a specific project. We then send an opt-in confirmation message such as: "You've been added to [Project Name] by Skyeline Homes. Reply YES to receive project updates or STOP to decline." SMS messaging only begins after you reply YES.
  3. Reply START: If you previously opted out, you may opt back in by texting START to our number.
Consent to receive SMS messages is never a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Skyeline Homes. All opt-in checkboxes default to unchecked, and SMS opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on project activity. During active phases of a project, you may receive multiple messages per day. During inactive periods you may receive none. We send only messages that are operationally relevant to your project or role.

5. How to Opt Out (STOP)

You can opt out of the SMS program at any time by replying STOP to any message we send you. After you reply STOP, we will send one final confirmation message and will not send further SMS messages to your number. Opt-out is honored immediately and your number is added to our suppression list.
