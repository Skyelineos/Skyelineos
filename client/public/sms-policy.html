Part 1 — SMS Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Skyeline Homes, LLC ("Skyeline Homes," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information from individuals who interact with our construction services and our SMS (text message) program. Skyeline Homes is a custom home building company based in American Fork, Utah, operating at skyelinehomes.com and skyeline-homes.com.

Mobile Information / SMS Consent

We collect your mobile phone number and SMS consent solely to send you project-related communications as described in this policy.

Mobile information (including your mobile phone number and your consent to receive SMS messages) will NOT be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of information may be shared with third parties only as described elsewhere in this policy.

Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number or messaging consent to any third party.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information — name, phone number, and email address provided when a client signs a construction contract or a subcontractor is onboarded to a project.
  • Project information — project name, address, scope of work, schedule, and your role on the project (client, subcontractor, vendor, inspector).
  • Communications — the content and timestamps of SMS messages exchanged between you and Skyeline Homes for project coordination.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate and coordinate active construction projects, including:

  • Sending project status updates (framing, concrete, inspections, milestones).
  • Scheduling and confirming on-site work, deliveries, and inspections.
  • Notifying you of task assignments and changes.
  • Sending payment, bid, and invoice notifications.
  • Responding to questions you send us by text.

3. SMS Messaging Program

By opting in to our SMS program (see the Terms of Service below for opt-in details), you agree to receive transactional and operational text messages from Skyeline Homes related to your project. Message frequency varies based on project activity. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You can request help by replying HELP. We honor opt-out requests immediately, and your number is added to our internal suppression list.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or phone number with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent records will not be shared with third parties.

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the SMS program and our project management platform, including:

  • Twilio — to send and receive SMS messages on our behalf.
  • Google Firebase — to securely store project data.
  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit) — for invoicing and bookkeeping.

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Skyeline Homes, our clients, or others.

5. Data Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) with role-based access controls. All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Access is limited to authorized Skyeline Homes personnel.

6. Data Retention

Project and communication data is retained for the duration of the project plus seven (7) years for financial and legal compliance. SMS opt-in consent records are retained for as long as you remain opted in, plus a reasonable period after opt-out for audit and compliance purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your personal data, or opt out of the SMS program, at any time by contacting us at tyler@skyelinehomes.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Skyeline Homes' services and SMS program are intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued participation in our SMS program after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Part 2 — SMS Terms of Service

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your participation in the SMS (text message) program operated by Skyeline Homes, LLC ("Skyeline Homes," "we," "us"). By opting in, you agree to these Terms and to the Privacy Policy above.

1. Program Description

Skyeline Homes is a custom home builder based in American Fork, Utah. Our SMS program is used to coordinate active construction projects with clients, subcontractors, vendors, and inspectors. Messages include:

  • Project status updates (framing, concrete, drywall, inspections, milestones).
  • Schedule confirmations and changes.
  • Material delivery confirmations.
  • Task assignments and reminders.
  • Inspection notices.
  • Bid, invoice, and payment notifications.

Messages may be sent in English or Spanish to accommodate our bilingual subcontractor workforce.

2. How to Opt In

You can opt in to receive SMS messages from Skyeline Homes through one of the following methods:

  1. Construction Contract: Clients sign a written construction contract that includes explicit SMS consent language. By signing and providing your mobile number, you acknowledge that you will receive project-related SMS updates from Skyeline Homes at that number. Opt-in consent is documented separately on the contract; the SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default and is never required as a condition of receiving construction services.
  2. Subcontractor Onboarding: Subcontractors provide their mobile number when being added to a specific project. We then send an opt-in confirmation message such as: "You've been added to [Project Name] by Skyeline Homes. Reply YES to receive project updates or STOP to decline." SMS messaging only begins after you reply YES.
  3. Reply START: If you previously opted out, you may opt back in by texting START to our number.
Consent to receive SMS messages is never a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Skyeline Homes. All opt-in checkboxes default to unchecked, and SMS opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on project activity. During active phases of a project, you may receive multiple messages per day. During inactive periods you may receive none. We send only messages that are operationally relevant to your project or role.

4. Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply based on your wireless carrier plan. Skyeline Homes is not responsible for any charges from your carrier. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to Opt Out (STOP)

You can opt out of the SMS program at any time by replying STOP to any message we send you. After you reply STOP, we will send one final confirmation message and will not send further SMS messages to your number. Opt-out is honored immediately and your number is added to our suppression list.

6. Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message to receive instructions and our contact information. For additional support, email tyler@skyelinehomes.com or call (208) 403-5905.

7. Supported Carriers

Our SMS program is compatible with major U.S. wireless carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, U.S. Cellular, and most regional carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Eligibility

You must be 18 years or older and the account holder (or have permission from the account holder) of the mobile number you provide. By opting in, you represent that you meet these requirements.

9. Changes to These Terms

We may update these Terms from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated by SMS or email to active program participants.

10. Privacy

Our handling of your information is described in the Privacy Policy above. We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or phone numbers with third parties for marketing.
