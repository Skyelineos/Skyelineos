Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Skyeline OS is a private platform operated by Skyeline Homes, LLC. This policy describes how we handle data within the platform.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information (name, email, phone) for authorized users
  • Project data (schedules, tasks, documents, photos)
  • Financial data (estimates, bids, invoices, payments) synchronized with QuickBooks Online
  • Communications (SMS, email threads related to projects)

2. How We Use Data

Data is used exclusively to manage construction projects, communicate with clients and subcontractors, and maintain accurate financial records for Skyeline Homes, LLC. We do not sell or share data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. QuickBooks Online Integration

Skyeline OS connects to QuickBooks Online via Intuit's OAuth 2.0 API. Financial data (invoices, bills, payments) flows between Skyeline OS and QuickBooks solely for the Company's internal accounting. We store OAuth tokens securely in Google Cloud Secret Manager and Firestore.

4. Data Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage) with role-based access controls. All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Retention

Project data is retained for the duration of the project plus seven (7) years for financial compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights

Authorized users may request access to or deletion of their personal data by contacting us directly.
